### 🔬 Research Interests & Focus Areas
- **Agentic AI & Neuro-Symbolic Systems:** Multi-agent orchestration, deterministic rule engines, and verifiable RAG pipelines for high-stakes domains (healthcare & finance).
- **Edge AI & On-Device Inference:** Quantized Small Language Models (SLMs), TensorFlow Lite / LiteRT optimization, and embedded ML inference on resource-constrained hardware (ESP32-S3, mobile).
- **Cross-Platform Systems Engineering:** High-performance native bindings (C++ FFI), real-time telemetry streaming, and offline-first architectures using Flutter & Android Native.
